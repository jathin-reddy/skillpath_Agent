# Problem Statement — EdTech & Future Skills

## Problem
Students and early-career learners struggle to translate a career goal into a personalized, prioritized learning path. They may have scattered skills, certificates, and projects but lack a systematic diagnosis of what is missing and what action should happen next.

## Users
- College students
- Final-year students preparing for placements
- Fresh graduates
- Learners transitioning toward a new technical role

## Why it matters
Generic course lists do not answer three critical questions:
1. What am I missing for my target role?
2. What should I learn first?
3. What evidence should I produce to prove readiness?

## Proposed outcome
A learner receives a prioritized skill-gap diagnosis, readiness score, 7-day action plan, practical assessment, and concrete next action.
